# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People reading essays closely and preparing revisions for themselves, another person, or an AI.

## Product Purpose

Annotate an immutable document in the browser and export complete, precisely located revision instructions. Documents stay on the device.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, precise, quiet. Institutional and clinical in tone: Arial/Helvetica UI, navy headings, Georgia for document text, Radix slate and indigo colors.

## Anti-references

The application must not become a document editor, AI chat interface, or upload service. Visual reference: dana-farber.org (typography and color only).

## Design Principles

- Preserve the original document and every annotation.
- Make location and scope explicit.
- Expose overlapping instructions without choosing for the user.
- Keep reading and annotation controls keyboard-accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Unicode and Chinese text, suppress shortcuts during IME composition, support keyboard annotation, and respect reduced motion. Do not rely on color alone to communicate overlapping notes.
